About & Privacy

How it works: rooms run directly between players' phones (peer-to-peer). There is no game account and no game server database. Your name, colour, photo, stickers and favourite memes are stored only in this browser.

Microphone: only used if you turn it on, and only streamed to players in your room. Nothing is recorded.

Network: to connect phones, your IP address is shared with the other players and with public connection (STUN/relay) services.

Be kind: chat, memes and stickers are visible to everyone in the room. The host can remove players and spectators. Don't share content you don't have the right to use or that could offend others.

Age: not intended for children under 13 to use without a parent's permission.

Official UNO! Rules

🎨 Strict Color & Number Matching

Yellow cards can only be played on Yellow, Red on Red, Blue on Blue, Green on Green, OR by matching the card number/symbol (e.g., 7 on 7).

⏱️ 10-Second Strict Timeout Auto-Play

If you don't take a move within 10 seconds, the AI engine will automatically evaluate your hand and play a valid matching card or draw for you following official UNO rules!

⚡ Action Card Rules

  • Skip: Skips next player's turn.
  • Reverse: Flips turn order direction.
  • Draw Two (+2): Next player draws 2 cards & forfeits turn.
  • Wild: Playable anytime to pick a new color.
  • Wild Draw Four (+4): Pick new color + next player draws 4 cards & forfeits turn.
